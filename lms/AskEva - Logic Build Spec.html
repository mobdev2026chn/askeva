Identity rule (resolves the duplication loop)

On every inbound, resolve phone → contactId first. If found, attach to the existing Contact and open a new Opportunity. Only mint a new Contact when the normalized phone is genuinely unseen. phone is unique; dedupe-on-ingest with a merge prompt enforces "one identity per human."

Why append-only

The event log doubles as the Customer Timeline the audit flagged as incomplete: render any contact's history by replaying their events in order. Reports and Dashboard become projections over the same log — no separate analytics plumbing, no drift.

PART 3

Triage rulebook

The decision table that ends "every agent classifies differently." Inbound intent → what AskEva creates → the AI's authority. Autonomy is the safety dial: Auto the AI acts alone, Propose it drafts and an agent approves, Human it must hand off.

Spam / invalidBot, gibberish, opt-outInvalidMark invalid; no promotionAuto
AI reply / hand-off rule

Reply when intent is known & autonomy is Auto and confidence ≥ threshold. Hand off on: low confidence, any Human intent, explicit "talk to a person," or a second unresolved loop. Every AI action emits an event for audit.

Conflict guards

One intent per message wins (highest-priority match). Complaint > Payment > Support > Booking > Pricing > Enquiry. A message can update at most one entity per turn to prevent the AI fanning out duplicates.

PART 4

State machines

Every entity gets one explicit state machine — no implicit transitions, no terminal dead-ends. This is where the audit's "close the dead-ends" and "post-sale lifecycle" steps become concrete.

Opportunity — the deal funnel
new →qualify hotwarmcold →close won / lost
Transitions

new → hot|warm|cold by qualification rule · hot↔warm↔cold freely re-qualify · any → won emits lead.won & creates/links Customer · any → lost requires lostReason & opens a win-back Flow. No state is terminal-without-exit — lost and won both route forward.

Customer — the post-sale lifecycle (the missing loop)
active ⇄inactivity / health at risk →lapse churned →win-back reactivated →opens new Opp active
The reactivation edge

This is the single most important missing transition from the audit's lifecycle diagram. A reactivated customer opens a new Opportunity against the existing Contact — it does not create a new Lead/Contact. That closes the duplication loop for good.

Ticket
open→ pending→ resolved
resolved→replyreopened→open

On resolved → optional ticket.followup_created (task or appointment). Reopen on customer reply within window.

Appointment
booked→completed
booked→rescheduled↺booked
booked→cancelled/no show

booked auto-schedules a reminder. cancelled → re-book prompt. no_show → follow-up task. No dead-ends.

PART 5

Phased build plan

The seven recommendations, sequenced so each phase unblocks the next and the readiness score climbs from 58 toward build-ready. Phase 0 is a hard gate — nothing module-specific ships until the entity model and identity rule exist.

steps 1 · 7
→ +14

Stand up Contact / Opportunity / Customer with FKs, unique normalized phone, and dedupe-on-ingest. Migrate the current "lead row" into the new graph.

Every record resolves to a stable contactId; no two rows share a phone; convert creates a Customer.

Owned automatic capture

step 2
→ +6

No inbound exists unowned past the grace window; lead.sla_breached escalates.

P2

Event bus

step 3
→ +4

Ship the append-only event log and the 30 typed events. Re-point Dashboard, Reports, and Notifications to subscribe rather than scan.

Customer Timeline renders purely by replaying events; no module re-derives state from chat text.

P3

Triage & AI boundaries

step 4
→ +5

Implement the triage decision table and the Auto / Propose / Human autonomy dial. AI actions route through ai.action_proposed for approval.

Every inbound intent maps to exactly one entity action with a defined AI authority and audit trail.

P4

Close dead-ends & lifecycle

steps 5 · 6
→ +5

Wire the no-show / cancel / resolved follow-up Flows, post-conversion onboarding, auto-reminders, and the Customer active→at-risk→churned→reactivated states with the reactivation edge.

No workflow terminates without a next step; returning customers re-engage instead of duplicating.
