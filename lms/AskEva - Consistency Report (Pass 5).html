Final consistency report

93/100

Production readiness: 93 / 100. Cohesive data model, live cross-module sync, single subscription store, and a unified timeline. Points held back for the event-sourced (not back-filled) ticket/appointment joins and the residual unused files.

Remaining defects

  • None blocking. Cross-module activity matches a lead by mobile/name; an interaction for a customer who is not yet a lead (or whose phone differs) won't appear on a lead timeline until that lead exists.

Remaining duplicate logic

  • None in the active app. The only duplication left is file-level: superseded module copies in the project root and uploads/ (see §7) — inert, recommended for archival.

Synchronisation gaps

  • Live ticket status on the lead: the timeline shows ticket events; it does not render a live list of a customer's open tickets joined by mobile. Additive follow-up if a "Tickets" tab on the lead detail is wanted.
  • Hidden-pane recompute: dash-perf recomputes on every leads:changed even when the Dashboard is off-screen — correct, marginally wasteful; could gate on visibility.

Performance recommendations

  • If lead volume grows past a few hundred, tag leads:changed with a source id to skip the self-render, and gate subscribers on pane visibility.
  • Consider a single shared askeva.* persistence helper to standardise the lone eva.wallpaper key.

Verified interactively this pass: Team mirrors the Agents store 1:1 (live two-way), leads:changed drives Dashboard KPIs without refresh, filters persist, and Chat / Appointments / Tickets all write to one per-lead activity timeline rendered on the lead detail.
