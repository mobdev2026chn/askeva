Executive summary

The Lead Configuration surface is broad and well-built visually — Reminders, Field & Dropdown configuration, Webhook, and Quick Reply are all present and interactive. The audit surfaced 8 issues, the most material being a taxonomy mismatch: the settings screens describe a lead pipeline and source vocabulary that no longer match what the live app actually uses. There is also no settings surface for Companies — they are silently derived from a free-text field. None of the findings are blockers, but the two high-severity items undermine trust in the configuration screens and should be fixed before any customer-facing rollout.

Scope & method

Reviewed every screen reachable from Lead Configuration (Reminders, Configuration → Lead Fields + Dropdown Fields, Webhook, Quick Reply), the Leads list filters, and the Companies tab. Each was checked for (a) functional correctness, (b) consistency with the live leads dataset and pipeline, and (c) UX/data-model gaps. Findings are rated High (breaks a user's mental model or produces wrong data), Medium (notable gap or friction), or Low (polish / nice-to-have).

Findings

HighConfiguration · Dropdown FieldsAE-01

Status taxonomy doesn't match the live pipeline

ObservedThe Status dropdown editor offers New · Hot · Warm · Cold · Converted · Invalid.
RealityThe leads list, dashboard, and detail pages run a 9-stage pipeline: New · Contacted · Qualified · Meeting Scheduled · Proposal Sent · Negotiation · Won · Lost · Follow-up Required.
ImpactEditing "Status" in settings has no effect on the real pipeline, and the labels shown are stale. Admins will believe they are configuring stages they cannot actually change.
RecommendDrive the Status dropdown from the single source of truth used by the leads module, and make it the real pipeline editor (rename/reorder/colour). Retire the legacy Hot/Warm/Cold labels.
HighConfiguration · Dropdown FieldsAE-02

Source vocabulary has drifted from the data

ObservedThe Source editor lists User Initiated - Whatsapp.
RealityThe app rebrands WhatsApp to "Chat" at runtime, so live records carry sources like Chat-Initiated and Chat-Sync. The settings list and the data no longer agree.
ImpactSource-based filtering and reporting can silently miss records whose stored value never appears in the configured list.
RecommendNormalise source labels in one place and seed the dropdown from observed values. Add a one-time migration note for older "Whatsapp" rows.
MediumCompaniesAE-03

No settings surface for Companies

ObservedCompanies are auto-grouped from the free-text Company field on each lead. There is no place to set an industry, owner, or to merge/rename/dedupe companies.
ImpactTypos fork a company into duplicates; there is no firmographic data to segment on; the Companies tab is read-only.
RecommendAdd a lightweight Company record (name, industry, owner, merge). Partially addressed in this cycle: the new Industry and Company filters introduce an industry taxonomy and a company picker — the natural next step is to make those editable rather than derived.
MediumConfiguration · Lead FieldsAE-04

Field-model rough edges

ObservedTags is typed as a single-line Input rather than a multi-value/tag control; Country Code and Mobile are split into two fields; the Product dropdown ships with zero options.
ImpactTags entered as free text won't chip cleanly; phone capture is more error-prone; an empty Product dropdown looks unfinished.
RecommendIntroduce a "Tags / Multi-select" field type, consider a combined phone+country control, and either seed Product options or hide the field until configured.
MediumWebhookAE-05

Webhook lacks validation & an accurate sample

ObservedThe URL field accepts any string; "Test Webhook" always reports success via a toast; the sample payload hard-codes a timestamp and uses "status": "New Lead", which isn't a real pipeline value.
ImpactUsers can save an invalid endpoint and believe it was verified; the sample misrepresents the actual payload shape.
RecommendValidate the URL, surface a real test result (status code / latency), and generate the sample from a live record so status values stay truthful.
LowQuick ReplyAE-06

Quick Replies have no categories or variables

ObservedQuick Replies are a flat list. The Chat module's templates, by contrast, support categories. There is no support for merge variables (e.g. lead name).
RecommendReuse the Chat template pattern: add categories and {{name}}-style variables for parity and faster reuse.
LowRemindersAE-07

Reminder labels & copy are ambiguous

Observed"Business Alert" is described with copy about tracking new leads in real time, which reads like the New-Lead alert beside it. The two toggles aren't clearly differentiated.
RecommendTighten each toggle's title + one-line description so the trigger (business hours vs. new-lead creation) is unambiguous.
LowAll settingsAE-08

Changes are session-only

ObservedEdits to fields, dropdowns, reminders, webhook and quick replies are held in memory and reset on reload.
ImpactExpected for the current prototype; flagged so it isn't mistaken for persisted behaviour during demos.
RecommendPersist to storage (or the API) when these screens graduate from prototype.
Addressed in this cycle

Alongside the audit, the Leads area gained a data-driven 30-Day Trend bar chart on the dashboard and a redesigned filter panel with Industry and Company filters, saved views, and polished active-filter chips. The Industry/Company work directly begins to close finding AE-03.

Prioritised recommendations

  • P0Unify the Status pipeline across settings and the live module; make the Status dropdown the real editor. (AE-01)
  • P0Reconcile Source labels with stored values and add a migration for legacy "Whatsapp" rows. (AE-02)
  • P1Promote Company to a real record with editable industry/owner and merge — building on the new Industry & Company filters. (AE-03)
  • P1Add a Tags/Multi-select field type and tidy the phone-number capture. (AE-04)
  • P1Make webhook testing real (validation + live test result + generated sample). (AE-05)
  • P2Bring Quick Replies to parity with Chat templates (categories + variables). (AE-06)
  • P2Clarify reminder copy and persist settings once out of prototype. (AE-07, AE-08)
